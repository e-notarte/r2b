Quadruple A Construction Conglomerate

R-II Builders is a Quadruple A, Platinum PCAB-accredited construction conglomerate with multibillion pesos worth of project accomplishments. The flagship company of the R-II Group, R-II Builders was incorporated on September 8, 1988. R-II Builders™ vast resources and over three decades of experience enable it to provide comprehensive housing and infrastructure solutions. R-II Builders™ construction capabilities span infrastructures across land and sea, and in support of air transport.

Vision

R-II Builders is the leader in monumental and sustainable infrastructure towards the socio-economic development of the nation.

Mission

Building better lives for Filipinos by creating quality infrastructures, mindful of the environment, employing competitive technological practices and adaptable to the fast-paced changing times.

What you can rely on

What is the solid foundation for every structure developed by R-II Builders? Beyond robust physical foundations, we are deeply grounded in our Philosophy: Building Better Lives. This commitment pervades our entire operations: as we serve public and private sector clients, investors, end-users, industry partners, and all other stakeholders.

Beyond structures

Our philosophy also powers our commitment to care for our people and for our communities through our corporate social responsibility work. We support community development, environmental protection, education (through scholarships and public school assistance), as well as disaster relief and response.
