Building the Nation's Infrastructure

What We Do

R-II Builders specializes in comprehensive housing and monumental infrastructure solutions. Our capabilities span across land, sea, and air transport infrastructures, delivering unparalleled quality and sustainability for over three decades.

Housing & Urban Development

Developing large-scale residential and urban communities that elevate the standard of living for Filipinos.

Land Infrastructure

Constructing vital highways, bridges, and road networks that connect cities and drive socio-economic growth.

Marine & Ports

Engineering advanced maritime facilities, ports, and sea transport infrastructure built to withstand the elements.

Air Transport Support

Providing robust structural and logistical support facilities for aviation and air transport networks.
